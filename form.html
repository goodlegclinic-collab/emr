一、監視錄影設備說明與同意書CCTV Equipment Notice & Consent

本診所於下列空間裝設有攝影監視錄影設備：
CCTV recording equipment is installed in the following areas:

  1. 公共空間Public areas

    包括前臺、走道等。Including reception, hallways, etc.

  2. 診間治療室內Consultation / treatment room

    攝影鏡頭針對門口，不會拍到私密部位，此設備係供維護病人安全與教學使用。The camera faces the doorway and does not capture private body parts; this equipment is used to safeguard patient safety and for teaching purposes.

  3. 更衣室與洗手間Changing room & restroom

    皆無安裝攝影設備。No recording equipment is installed.

二、病灶處影像使用同意Consent for Use of Lesion-Site Images

醫療過程中，醫師可能就病灶部位（不涉及含可辨識身份的臉部）拍攝照片或影像，並就下列用途使用：
During care, the physician may photograph the lesion site (excluding the identifiable face) and use the images for the following purposes:

  1. 病歷紀錄與醫療診治Medical records & treatment

    用於病情評估、治療前後對照及病歷存檔。For assessment, before/after comparison, and archiving.

  2. 院內學術研究與教學Academic research & teaching

    去識別化後用於院內教育訓練、學術研究、個案討論。De-identified, for internal training, research, and case discussion.

  3. 衛教與行銷宣傳Health education & promotion

    去識別化後用於衛教文章、社群、官網等對外宣傳。De-identified, for articles, social media, and website.
